# Northwold Local Plan 2027 to 2042

> Summary of the proposed local plan, January 2028. Fictional sample made for the Local Plan Navigator: Northwold District Council and its places do not exist.

Notes: This deck summarises the proposed local plan for councillors and partners. It is a fictional sample, deliberately incomplete in places, for trying the plan checker on a presentation.

---

# What the plan does

- Covers 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2042; adoption expected in spring 2029
- Provides at least 9,600 homes (640 a year) and 32 hectares of employment land
- Replaces the Northwold Core Strategy (2014) and the Site Allocations Plan (2016) in full
- Allocates five sites, including a garden village at Harrowfield

Notes: There is no spatial development strategy for Northwold yet. The Fenland and Wolds Strategic Authority expects to publish a draft in 2029.

---

# Our vision for 2042

- People of every age can find a home they can afford, close to work, services and green space
- Wendbury grows to the north, and its town centre and the Maltings come back to life
- A new garden village at Harrowfield, with schools, jobs and a frequent bus to Wendbury station
- The Northwold Wolds, the River Wend and the Fens protected and richer in wildlife
- New development net zero carbon and safe from flooding

# Spatial strategy

| Place | Role | Share of new homes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wendbury | Main town | 60% |
| Harrowfield Garden Village | New settlement | 16% |
| Coldmarsh and Saxholme | Market towns | 17% |
| Little Harrow and other villages | Villages | 7% |

Notes: The combination of Wendbury and a new settlement was chosen from four options tested in the Sustainability Appraisal, because Wendbury has the only railway station and its growth beyond 2035 is limited by the floodplain and the Wolds.

# Policy NW/GV1: Harrowfield Garden Village

- About 1,900 homes, 8 hectares of employment land, two primary schools, a secondary school and a health centre
- On the former RAF Harrowfield airfield and farmland; first homes in 2032, about 1,500 by 2042
- A new junction on the Wend Valley Road before the 200th home is occupied
- A bus every 15 minutes to Wendbury station from the first occupation
- The upgrade of the Wendbury water recycling centre funded before the 500th home

---

# Policy NW1: Sustainable development

- The Council will take a positive approach that reflects the presumption in favour of sustainable development in the National Planning Policy Framework
- Applications that accord with the local plan will be approved without delay, unless material considerations indicate otherwise

Notes: Officers have asked whether this policy is still needed under the August 2026 NPPF.

---

# Policy NW5: Affordable housing

- On sites of 10 or more homes: 35% affordable in Wendbury and Harrowfield
- Between 20% and 30% affordable in Coldmarsh, Saxholme and the villages
- 60% social rent and 40% affordable home ownership, reflecting the Local Housing Needs Assessment (2025)

---

# Policy NW10: Biodiversity

- All development must deliver at least 10% biodiversity net gain, on site where possible
- In the Wend valley and the Wolds, priority areas in the Wendshire Local Nature Recovery Strategy (2025), major development must deliver at least 20% net gain
